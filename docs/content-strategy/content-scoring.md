# Content Scoring

## Opportunity score

Suggested weighting:
- Commercial intent: 25%
- Ranking probability: 20%
- Existing topical authority: 15%
- Traffic potential: 15%
- Affiliate value: 15%
- Internal-link value: 10%

## Outcome

The score should help prioritize what to build next, but should not override obvious strategic conflicts such as cannibalization or lack of monetization.

## Revenue feedback loop

Track by URL:
- clicks
- impressions
- rankings
- affiliate clicks
- conversions
- revenue
- RPM
- email opt-ins

Use actual revenue data to increase investment in winning clusters.
