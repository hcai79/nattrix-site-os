# Content Strategy Overview

## Goal

Build focused topical authority around electronics test equipment and workbench tools, then monetize that authority through commercial buying guides, product profiles, affiliate offers, ads, sponsorships, email, and future digital products.

## Positioning

CircuitsAtHome helps makers, hobbyists, students, and technicians choose, understand, and use the right electronics tools and test equipment.

## Initial authority focus

- Oscilloscopes
- Multimeters
- Soldering and rework
- Logic analyzers
- Bench power supplies
- Electronics workbench setup

## Core principle

Informational content should answer real user questions first, then naturally route qualified visitors toward the most relevant Core commercial page and product records.

The initial system should optimize for revenue per page and durable topical authority, not publishing volume.
