# Publishing Rules

## Cluster-based publishing

Do not publish random articles across unrelated topics.

Preferred sequence:
1 Core commercial page
+
4-8 tightly related supporting pieces

## Human approval

Always required for:
- new Core commercial pages
- major product recommendations
- major comparative claims
- sponsored content
- substantial visual claims or technical diagrams that could be misleading if wrong

## Automation candidates

May become automated after QA:
- support article drafts
- metadata
- schema
- internal-link recommendations
- image brief generation
- simple non-technical visual concepts
- refresh detection
