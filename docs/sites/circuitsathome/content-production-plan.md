# CircuitsAtHome Content Production Plan

## Scope

This plan contains 30 initial commercial pages and 100 supporting articles across six authority waves.

## Publishing waves

1. Oscilloscopes
2. Multimeters
3. Soldering and Rework
4. Bench Power Supplies
5. Logic Analyzers
6. Electronics Workbench

## Default support article model

Unless overridden in the CSV:
- page type: Support Article
- intent: Informational
- funnel: TOFU/MOFU
- monetization: display ads plus affiliate assist
- internal linking: primary Core target plus relevant sibling support content
- visuals: explanatory diagrams, comparison graphics, checklists, or flowcharts where they improve comprehension

## Production rule

Do not publish all 130 pages at once. Build one cluster deeply, measure rankings and revenue, and use performance to influence later waves.

## Core page approval

All 30 commercial pages require human approval before publication.

## Visual rule

Technical diagrams and factual infographics must use the approved research pack. Safety-sensitive measurement or wiring visuals require QA and human review.
