# CircuitsAtHome Positioning

CircuitsAtHome helps makers, hobbyists, students, and technicians choose, understand, and use the right electronics tools and test equipment.

## Focus

- electronics test equipment
- soldering and rework
- bench equipment
- practical workbench setup

## Not the initial focus

- electrician directory
- EV charger installers
- smart-home installer directory
- general consumer electronics
- broad phone/TV/computer content
- broad Arduino/Raspberry Pi content unrelated to tools
- news
