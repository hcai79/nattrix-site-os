# CircuitsAtHome

## Positioning

CircuitsAtHome should become a focused authority for electronics workbench tools, test equipment, and practical DIY electronics guidance.

## Primary ICP

Serious DIY makers, electronics hobbyists, engineering students, technicians, and advanced beginners who actively buy equipment and components.

## Core commercial focus

Initial clusters:
- oscilloscopes
- multimeters
- soldering equipment
- logic analyzers
- bench power supplies

## Monetization priority

1. Affiliate product commissions
2. Display ads on supporting informational content
3. Manufacturer sponsorships
4. Featured product placements
5. Email monetization
6. Digital products / education

## Content model

Hub -> Core commercial page -> Product records -> Supporting content

Supporting articles should have a clear relationship to a Core page where relevant.

## Automation target

High automation:
- opportunity discovery
- briefs
- support content drafts
- schema
- internal-link suggestions
- refresh detection

Human review:
- Core commercial pages
- final product recommendations
- sponsored content
- major architecture changes

## Initial success metric

Prove that the platform can:
1. track the Core 30,
2. identify support opportunities,
3. draft and QA support content,
4. route traffic to commercial pages,
5. measure revenue per URL.
