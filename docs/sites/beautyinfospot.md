# BeautyInfoSpot | National Med Spa Directory

## Vision
Build a US-wide discovery and consultation marketplace for med spas and nonsurgical aesthetic treatments. Launch market-by-market; never equate national architecture with immediate national coverage.

## Audience
- Consumers researching local aesthetic services, prices, qualifications, and consultation options.
- Clinic owners seeking qualified prospective customers and transparent advertising opportunities.

## Positioning
"Compare med spas and aesthetic treatments, review provider information, and request consultations with confidence." No medical endorsement or clinical vetting implied by an ordinary listing.

## Core value
Verified factual business information, useful local comparisons, transparent paid placements, consent-based consultation requests, and educational treatment content with qualified medical review.

## Launch strategy
- Pilot: Las Vegas metro (including Henderson and Summerlin as relevant geographic filters).
- Initial inventory: 20-30 source-checked profiles; 3-5 treatment categories.
- National taxonomy and URL conventions from day one, but publish city/service pages only where verified coverage supports a useful result.
- Next markets chosen by consumer demand, provider density, business willingness to pay, competition, and ability to verify inventory.

## Revenue hypotheses (validate, do not treat as forecasts)
- Free basic listing
- Featured listing: test $149-249/month
- Premium listing/promotion: test $299-499/month
- Accepted qualified inquiry: test $40-100; explicit lead-quality definition and consumer consent required
- City/category sponsorships later; clearly label sponsored placements
- Optional local SEO services through separate contracts

## Minimum viable product
1. National treatment hub and med spa directory
2. State/city browsing with searchable, verified provider profiles
3. Treatment-specific city landing pages only when helpful and sufficiently populated
4. Claim listing request, with ownership verification before edits
5. Consultation inquiry routed to selected clinic with explicit consent
6. Sponsored listing labeling and transparent ranking methodology
7. Admin approval and audit log
8. Analytics: visits, profile views, inquiry submits, accepted leads, advertiser revenue, revenue per visitor

## Page templates
### Directory city hub
Unique intro; verified provider count; treatment filters; provider cards with source dates; location details; disclosure of paid ranking; FAQs; local navigation; consultation CTA.
### Provider profile
Business name; address; phone; official site; hours (source and checked date); services confirmed; practitioner credentials with independent verification status; pricing only when sourced; photos only with rights; claim status; last reviewed date; sponsored label; request consultation; correction request.
### Treatment hub
Plain-language overview; benefits and limitations; eligibility and safety questions; price variability; what to ask providers; reviewed-by credential where applicable; related providers and locations; references.
### Treatment-city page
Verified local inventory; treatment availability; local considerations; transparent listing order; no invented costs or fabricated 'best' ratings.
### For clinics
Free listing, verification, paid promotion terms, inquiry definition, billing, refund/dispute rules, lead response expectations.

## SEO and structured data
- Site-wide breadcrumbs and canonical URLs.
- Appropriate LocalBusiness / MedicalBusiness structured data only for verified facts and valid subtype; never invent ratings or claim Google review stars.
- Index only useful populated location/service pages; noindex empty filters and thin generated permutations.
- Strong internal linking: treatment -> location -> provider -> treatment guides.
- Human medical review for clinical claims, with review dates and corrections process.
- Sponsored placements separated from editorial selection; FTC-compliant disclosures.

## Success gates
Pilot is ready when 20+ verified profiles, functional inquiry consent and routing, 3 treatment hubs, location hub, claim workflow, tracking, and a clinic feedback loop are live.
Expand only after measurable qualified leads and evidence of advertiser willingness to pay.
