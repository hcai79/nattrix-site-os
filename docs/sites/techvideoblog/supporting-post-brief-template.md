## Scope

Explain the practical question this Post answers. State why it is distinct from the
linked Core page and from nearby published Posts. It must teach a workflow, checklist,
decision framework, or implementation step rather than reproduce a commercial ranking.

## Research pack

Provide before drafting:

1. Three to five primary or authoritative sources relevant to factual claims.
2. A source URL and checked date for every product, pricing, policy, testing, or
   compatibility claim.
3. A short list of statements that remain unresolved. Do not fill gaps from memory.
4. A note describing the reader situation, examples, and terminology used in the draft.

## Required outline

1. A direct answer or outcome-oriented introduction.
2. The workflow, checklist, or decision criteria.
3. Practical steps with constraints and realistic tradeoffs.
4. A short section explaining when the approach is not a fit.
5. One contextual route to the Primary Core target.
6. A conclusion that helps the reader choose a next step without repeating the Core
   page's rankings.

## Internal-link rule

Add one natural, descriptive link to the assigned Primary Core target. Do not force
additional commercial links, change existing URLs, or include affiliate URLs in the
draft.

## Completion checklist

- The draft matches the assigned intent and does not overlap a known Core page.
- Every material factual claim has a source or is removed or marked for review.
- No pricing, free-plan, performance, "tested," or product-availability claim is made
  without current primary-source evidence.
- The draft has a clear H1, useful H2 structure, and no empty or repeated sections.
- The output includes the sources, claim notes, proposed internal-link anchor, and any
  question requiring owner review.
- Status changes to **Review**. The VA does not publish or alter the live site.
