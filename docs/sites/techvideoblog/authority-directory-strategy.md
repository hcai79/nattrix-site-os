# TechVideoBlog Authority Directory Strategy

## Positioning

Help creators select AI video tools through evidence-backed tool profiles, comparisons, use-case pages, and practical tutorials.

## Durable content paths

1. Tool profile: what it does, best for, tradeoffs, price context, evidence, alternatives, and related workflows.
2. Category hub: user job, selection criteria, tool cards, category-specific comparisons, and links to profiles.
3. Use-case page: audience, task, workflow, recommended fit, limitations, and links to the relevant tools.
4. Comparison: decision criteria, honest winner-by-use-case guidance, price context, and alternatives.
5. Tutorial: practical completion path, tool dependencies, and natural links into relevant profiles or categories.

## Quality floor for commercial pages

Every published commercial page should have a clear verdict, best-for and not-best-for guidance, current pricing or a dated pricing caveat, methodology, related internal links, FAQ, and links to the site testing and affiliate-disclosure pages.

## Prioritization formula

Prioritize pages by: commercial intent, existing authority, evidence availability, search demand, internal-link value, freshness risk, and conversion opportunity. Do not prioritize publishing volume alone.
