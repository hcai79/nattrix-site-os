# Tool Review QA Scorecard

Use this scorecard for a read-only review of a TechVideoBlog tool page before preparing any staging brief. It is designed for the review template used by CapCut, VEED, and other future tool pages.

## Guardrails

- Do not edit the live page while performing this scorecard.
- Do not change URL, canonical, redirects, taxonomy, affiliate treatment, imagery, navigation, or global styling.
- Do not use a vendor marketing claim as evidence of hands-on testing.
- Record sources in the Operations Hub Evidence Ledger before proposing a factual refresh.
- Escalate broken routes, ambiguous affiliate disclosures, safety-sensitive claims, and consent or likeness claims instead of guessing.

## Scorecard

| Area | Pass criteria | Evidence to retain | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision opening | States who the tool fits and who should choose an alternative. | Current page excerpt and the related alternative pages. | 0 to 10 |
| Tool facts | Pricing, plans, limits, platforms, and feature statements have current primary sources. | Official price, product, and help URLs with retrieval date and billing context. | 0 to 20 |
| Policy facts | Privacy, data use, AI training, retention, consent, and commercial-use claims are sourced or marked unresolved. | Official privacy, terms, policy, or help URLs. | 0 to 15 |
| Editorial clarity | Manufacturer claims, editorial assessments, and verified test observations are clearly distinguished. | Dated internal test record for any tested or verified wording. | 0 to 15 |
| Buyer guidance | Best-for, not-best-for, pros, cons, and alternatives align with the verified facts. | Claim-to-source mapping and relevant review or comparison URLs. | 0 to 10 |
| Trust and monetization | Methodology and disclosure links work; affiliate status is clear and unchanged. | Link check result and page excerpt. | 0 to 10 |
| Internal discovery | Supporting review, category, comparison, and use-case links resolve and serve reader intent. | Destination list and HTTP result. | 0 to 10 |
| Structure and accessibility | Clear headings, scannable sections, descriptive link text, and no broken in-page navigation. | Heading map and link map. | 0 to 10 |

Maximum score: 100.

## Decision rules

| Result | Meaning | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90 to 100 and no critical gap | Strong evidence-backed candidate. | Prepare a narrow staging brief for human approval. |
| 70 to 89 | Structure is usable but evidence or clarity gaps remain. | Complete the missing Evidence Ledger records before drafting. |
| Below 70 | Material factual, policy, structure, or link gaps. | Create a page-specific repair brief. Do not draft live changes. |
| Any critical issue | Broken internal destination, uncertain canonical or affiliate treatment, unsupported testing claim, or unresolved policy-sensitive guidance. | Stop that affected change and record an owner decision or research task. |

## Required handoff packet

1. Page ID, URL, retrieval date, word count, and heading map.
2. Filled scorecard with concise evidence notes.
3. Source ledger IDs for every retained price, plan-limit, policy, and product-capability claim.
4. Internal-link check results, including redirect destinations.
5. A scoped staging proposal that names blocks or sections to change, but does not alter the published page.
6. Explicit statement of what remains unresolved and whether owner approval is required.
