# Model Benchmarks and Promotion Rules

Do not guess the cheapest/best model from marketing or token pricing. Benchmark candidate models for each job.

## Fixed evaluation set
- 10 keyword clustering/duplicate pairs (including near-duplicates)
- 10 product specification extraction records with authoritative source text
- 5 competitor analysis packets with dated URLs
- 5 supporting-article briefs and resulting drafts
- 5 commercial product comparison fact packs
- 5 factual-graphic briefs containing precise values
- 10 invalid/ambiguous cases that must trigger escalation

## Metrics
- schema-valid response rate
- factual claim accuracy and citation traceability
- editorial acceptance rate
- reviewer correction minutes
- reliability and latency
- cost per accepted result inclusive of retries and retrieval
- privacy suitability

## Selection
1. Qualify models by modality/schema/context/approved-provider policy.
2. Reject models below required accuracy or acceptance thresholds.
3. Rank the rest by full cost per accepted result.
4. Deploy an allowlisted champion for each task; shadow test cheaper challengers.
5. Require human sign-off before swapping models for technical QA or Core commercial recommendations.

Keep the evaluation corpus, scoring scripts and outcomes versioned. Do not send private client content to model providers that fail the project's privacy policy.
