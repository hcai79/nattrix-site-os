# BeautyInfoSpot | 12-Week Launch Roadmap

## Week 1 | Audit and decision
Audit live BeautyInfoSpot WordPress site and existing URLs. Inspect Nattrix plugin and migrations. Select GeoDirectory vs custom implementation. Define taxonomy and listing evidence policy. Interview 5 clinics.

## Week 2 | Data foundations
Create schema and approval statuses. Configure staging WordPress, treatments and locations. Interview 5 more clinics. Establish privacy and consent language for review.

## Week 3 | Templates
Build city, treatment, business profile and clinic signup templates. Add admin approval, dedupe, audit history.

## Week 4 | First inventory
Collect 50 candidate businesses; verify and approve 20 profiles. Publish Las Vegas directory hub. Launch event tracking.

## Week 5 | Consultation flow
Add clinic inquiry forms, consent capture, anti-spam, lead delivery and GoHighLevel tracking.

## Week 6 | Treatment content
Publish 3 medically reviewed treatment hubs and useful internal links. Verify provider service availability.

## Week 7 | Clinic outreach
Send individually reviewed listing-claim invitations; test listing and lead pricing with interested clinics.

## Week 8 | Inventory and UX
Expand to 30-50 verified listings if sourcing permits; improve filters, mobile UX, and accessibility.

## Week 9 | Monetization pilot
Offer clearly labeled paid featured placements to consenting clinics; track leads and acceptance.

## Week 10 | SEO QA
Inspect Search Console, canonicalization, indexation, thin pages, structured data and conversion paths.

## Week 11 | Unit economics
Review qualified inquiries, clinic conversion, revenue, costs, lead disputes, and data freshness.

## Week 12 | Expansion decision
Choose next city only if provider supply, consumer demand, operational QA, and advertiser willingness to pay support it.

## Weekly scorecard
Verified businesses; published profiles; business claims; approved treatment pages; organic sessions; profile-to-inquiry rate; consented inquiries; accepted leads; active advertisers; MRR; revenue per visitor; data correction backlog.

## Definition of done
A launch-ready pilot has real sourced profiles, functioning consumer inquiry routing, consent logs, verified treatment categories, disclosures, audit logs, a correction channel, and tested WordPress/Supabase synchronization.
