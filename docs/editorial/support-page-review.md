# Support Page Review

## Goal

Keep review fast while catching factual or structural problems.

## Fast review checklist

- correct intent
- no duplicate target
- accurate key facts
- natural writing
- relevant Core link
- useful internal links
- no unsupported personal-experience claims
- visuals accurate
- CTA not forced
- no obvious filler

## Initial standard

Human review required for all support pages during the first 50 published articles.

## Graduated automation

After sufficient quality data exists:
- low-risk support pages may be approved by sampling
- medium-risk pages remain human-reviewed
- technical/safety pages always receive human review

## Sampling rule

Automation should only increase after:
- low correction rate
- low factual error rate
- stable SEO QA scores
- no recurring reviewer complaint category

Do not automate simply because article volume is high.
