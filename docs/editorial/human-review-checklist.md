# Human Review Checklist

## Reviewer goal

Confirm that the article is trustworthy, useful, commercially appropriate, and publication-ready.

The reviewer should not rewrite the entire article unless necessary.

## Universal checks

### Accuracy
- Are factual claims supported?
- Are technical specs correct?
- Are numbers, dimensions, ratings, and compatibility details verified?
- Are dates and prices treated as time-sensitive when appropriate?

### Integrity
- Does the article avoid implying hands-on testing that did not occur?
- Are subjective statements clearly editorial rather than factual?
- Are manufacturer claims identified where appropriate?

### Search intent
- Does the page answer the query directly?
- Is it materially different from existing pages?
- Is there cannibalization risk?
- Is the page type correct?

### Usefulness
- Is the introduction concise?
- Are important questions answered?
- Is the content practical?
- Are there unnecessary filler sections?

### Commercial fit
- Are product recommendations appropriate for the user?
- Are CTAs natural?
- Is monetization secondary to usefulness?
- Are affiliate disclosures present where needed?

### Internal linking
- Does the article link to its intended Core page?
- Are sibling links helpful and not forced?

### Visual review
- Are technical visuals accurate?
- Are captions and alt text useful?
- Are visuals informative rather than decorative?

### Final quality
- Does it sound natural?
- Are repeated AI phrases removed?
- Is formatting clean?
- Would a knowledgeable reader trust it?

## Reviewer should focus attention on

1. Low-confidence claims
2. Recommendations
3. Product comparisons
4. Safety-sensitive instructions
5. Pricing or availability
6. Technical visuals
7. Claims flagged by QA

## Reviewer should avoid

- rewriting for stylistic preference only
- expanding content without search/user value
- introducing unsupported claims
- changing URLs without approval
