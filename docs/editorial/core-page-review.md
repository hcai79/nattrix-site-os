# Core Page Review

## Required review depth

Core commercial pages require full human approval.

## Required checks

### Product selection
- Are the recommended products genuinely appropriate?
- Are products available?
- Are products sufficiently differentiated?
- Are there obvious missing candidates?

### Evidence
- Are specifications sourced?
- Are comparative statements supportable?
- Are editorial judgments distinguishable from verified facts?

### Commercial quality
- Is the page useful before affiliate clicks?
- Are recommendations segmented by use case?
- Is the comparison table accurate?
- Are products ranked for fit rather than payout?

### Cannibalization
- Does another page already satisfy the same intent?
- Should this page be merged?

### Visuals
- Are comparison graphics correct?
- Do diagrams reflect verified specs?
- Are exact product images sourced rather than fabricated?

### CTA
- Does each CTA match the surrounding intent?
- Is there excessive affiliate placement?

## Required reviewer outcome

APPROVE, RETURN, ESCALATE, or CONSOLIDATE.

Do not auto-publish Core pages.
