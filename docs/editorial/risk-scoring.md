# Editorial Risk Scoring

## Purpose

Determine how much human review a page requires.

## Risk dimensions

Score each 0-3:

### Commercial impact
0 none
1 minor
2 meaningful
3 high-revenue Core page

### Technical complexity
0 simple
1 moderate
2 technical
3 safety-sensitive

### Claim uncertainty
0 fully sourced
1 minor ambiguity
2 editorial judgment heavy
3 unresolved/weak sourcing

### Visual risk
0 none
1 decorative
2 factual infographic
3 wiring/safety/technical

### Change risk
0 new low-stakes page
1 normal update
2 high-ranking page update
3 URL/canonical/architecture impact

## Review bands

### 0-3 Low
Light human review.

### 4-7 Medium
Standard human review.

### 8-11 High
Deep human review.

### 12-15 Critical
Owner/domain-expert review required.

## Automation rule

No article bypasses human review solely because the AI QA score is high. Risk band and proven historical correction rate both matter.
