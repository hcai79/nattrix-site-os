# Fact and Claim Confidence

## Purpose

Direct human attention toward uncertain or subjective content.

## Claim classes

### Verified Fact
Directly supported by a strong source.

Example:
"Model X has four channels."

### Derived Fact
Calculated or inferred from verified source data.

Example:
"Two units together provide eight total channels."

### Editorial Assessment
A reasoned recommendation.

Example:
"Best choice for most beginners."

### Manufacturer Claim
Promotional or performance claim from the manufacturer.

Example:
"Designed for ultra-low-noise measurements."

### Community Sentiment
Observed user opinion.

Example:
"Frequently praised for its interface."

## Confidence score

### 95-100
Primary source, unambiguous.

### 80-94
Strong source, minor interpretation.

### 60-79
Editorial judgment or multiple secondary sources.

### Below 60
Do not publish without additional verification or explicit qualification.

## Human review triggers

Always surface:
- confidence below 80
- editorial superlatives
- comparative claims
- safety instructions
- product performance claims
- pricing claims
- technical measurements
- visual claims
