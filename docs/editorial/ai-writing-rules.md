# AI Writing Rules

## Writing principles

- direct
- practical
- specific
- natural
- beginner-friendly when appropriate
- technically responsible
- concise where possible
- no keyword stuffing

## Never imply unsupported hands-on experience

Do not use:
- "we tested"
- "our testing showed"
- "after months of use"
- "we found"
- "I personally recommend"

unless verified first-hand evidence exists in the project.

## Recommendation language

Prefer:
- "stands out for"
- "is a strong fit for"
- "makes sense for"
- "offers"
- "is better suited to"

Avoid unsupported absolutes:
- "most reliable"
- "best quality"
- "never fails"
- "perfect"

## Introductions

Avoid generic openings.

Start with:
- direct answer
- buying distinction
- problem framing
- decision insight

## Commercial content

Recommendation order should reflect:
1. user fit
2. verified capabilities
3. tradeoffs
4. price/value
5. affiliate opportunity

Never reverse this priority.

## Support content

Answer the query before introducing commercial links.

## Draft requirements

Writer must identify:
- unresolved factual gaps
- low-confidence statements
- suggested visuals
- Core page relationship
- monetization opportunities
