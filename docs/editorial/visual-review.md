# Visual Review

## Purpose

Ensure images, infographics, diagrams, and charts are accurate and useful.

## Review categories

### Informational
- does the visual make the concept clearer?
- does it accurately represent the text?

### Technical
- are labels correct?
- are measurements correct?
- are connections correct?
- are signal shapes or diagrams accurate?

### Product
- does the visual represent the real product accurately?
- does it avoid inventing ports, controls, or features?

### Comparison
- are all values verified?
- are scales fair?
- are subjective labels clearly editorial?

### Accessibility
- meaningful alt text
- readable labels
- not dependent on visual alone for key information

## Mandatory human review

- wiring diagrams
- electrical measurement diagrams
- safety diagrams
- factual comparison graphics
- branded/sponsored infographics
- schematics

## Generated imagery

Generated illustrations are appropriate for:
- concepts
- workflows
- generic workbench scenes
- abstract hero art
- educational visualizations

Do not use generated imagery as a substitute for an exact real-product image when product identification matters.
