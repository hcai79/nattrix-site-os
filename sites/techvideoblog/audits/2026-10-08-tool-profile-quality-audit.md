## Freshness and claim review

The sampled pages were last modified between May 10 and May 16, 2026. Their Rank Math titles and descriptions include time-sensitive statements such as pricing, testing, and hands-on claims. Examples include a claim of a 2026 tested review and a claim of testing more than 300 Shorts.

These statements are not treated as inaccurate by this audit. They need a source or internal testing record before any refresh keeps, expands, or restates them.

## Safe upgrade brief

For each profile, prepare an evidence pack covering current plan names, prices, free-plan limits, key workflow limitations, supported platforms, and the basis for any testing claim. Then prepare a draft that adds:

1. A concise verdict.
2. Supported best-for and not-best-for guidance.
3. A dated pricing or free-plan note.
4. A useful FAQ only for actual decision questions.
5. Intent-safe alternatives and comparison links.

Do not change production copy, SEO titles, descriptions, or claims until source evidence and owner approval are available.
